📌 Who We Are

📂 Our Open-Source Projects

All projects are built on the Pi Network blockchain and are publicly available on GitHub under the MIT License.

---

1. AJYAL – Decentralized Education Platform

Repository: github.com/mibo01699/AJYAL

A decentralized, smart, empowering educational platform targeting children, youth, and women in conflict zones. Features an educational incentive system with micro-rewards in YER tokens, management of in-kind assistance codes for people with special needs, and integration with BIGISH-YER and GAV.

Key Features:

· Free blockchain-based learning platform with offline capability
· Micro-grant system for students (YER tokens)
· Verified credential issuance on-chain
· Dedicated support for people with special needs

---

2. BIGISH-YER – Financial Infrastructure

Repository: github.com/mibo01699/BIGISH-YER

The backbone of economic stabilization in Yemen through Pi Network infrastructure. Provides digital wallets, financial transfers, salary payments, and humanitarian aid distribution via smart contracts.

Key Features:

· Digital wallet system (YER token)
· Individual and batch transfers (/api/yer/transfer, /api/yer/batch-transfer)
· Automated payroll processing
· Integration with AJYAL (clearing system) and GAV (point-of-sale)
· Anti-fraud mechanisms (AntiDoubleDippingEngine.js)

---

3. GAV – The Incense Route – Supply Chain & Trade

Repository: github.com/mibo01699/GAV-The-Incense-Route

A hybrid barter platform for cross-border logistics, combining escrow smart contracts, ERC-3643 tokenized real assets, and a dual-wallet split payment engine (Pi + YER).

Key Features:

· Hybrid payment system (Pi + YER)
· Hybrid Point-of-Sale (POS) terminal
· Supply chain tracking and verification
· GCV (Global Consensus Value) anchoring

---

4. Suppliers Auction – Decentralized Bidding Protocol

Repository: github.com/mibo01699/suppliers-auction

A decentralized, globally scalable bidding protocol for transparent, fair, and automated bidding between suppliers and buyers. Architected for dual-chain settlement (EVM + Pi Network).

Key Features:

· Smart contract-based auction management
· Hybrid settlement (Pi + YER)
· KYC-enforced participation (Pi SDK Authentication)
· 3D shipment tracking
· KYB (Know Your Business) for suppliers and logistics providers

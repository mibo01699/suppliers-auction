**Version:** 1.0.0

## 🛡️ 1. Liability Waiver

The applications, software architectures, and repositories within this ecosystem operate exclusively as decentralized peer-to-peer (P2P) technology mediation service providers. The platform, its core management, parent entities, individual developers, and network service providers do not represent buyers, suppliers, merchants, or institutional entities.

All users hereby grant an irrevocable waiver releasing the platform from any civil, criminal, administrative, or financial liabilities arising from fraud, misrepresentation, or breaches of contracts executed between transacting peers.

---

## 💰 2. Compliance with AML/CFT

The platform operates in alignment with international anti-money laundering and counter-terrorism financing (AML/CFT) frameworks.

- **Asset Freeze:** The protocol may temporarily freeze escrow pools upon detection of suspicious activity, pending review.
- **Identity Enforcement:** The platform may disclose transaction metadata to competent international law enforcement when compelled by a valid judicial order.

---

## ⚖️ 3. International Arbitration

All disputes shall be settled exclusively by Binding Mandatory Arbitration administered by the **International Chamber of Commerce (ICC)**.

- **Seat:** Geneva, Switzerland or London, United Kingdom.
- **Language:** English exclusively.
- **Class Action Waiver:** No user may file or join a class action lawsuit against the platform.

---

## 📜 4. Governing Law

This protocol shall be governed by International Commercial Law and the General Principles of International Economic Law (Lex Mercatoria), superseding any conflicting domestic statutes.

---

## 🛑 5. Force Majeure

The platform assumes no liability for service interruptions caused by:
- Sovereign state interventions
- Geopolitical embargoes
- Infrastructure degradation
- Updates to underlying blockchain protocols
